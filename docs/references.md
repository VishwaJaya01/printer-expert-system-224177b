# References and knowledge acquisition

Publisher: Microsoft Support. Access date for S01-S11: 28 September 2026. The scope is Windows 11. No interview or physical printer experiment was conducted.

The articles supply the domain knowledge. Their checks were converted into a small vocabulary of observations, 30 finding-to-remedy facts, and 30 conditional production rules. Conditions are deliberately limited to observable states; the program does not turn a possible cause into a confirmed diagnosis. R31 is the general logical join between findings and remedy facts. Full source articles are linked rather than reproduced.
